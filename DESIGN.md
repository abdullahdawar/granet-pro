## Visual Theme
SyncFlow uses a pristine, high-contrast SaaS aesthetic. It relies on clean whitespace, crisp borders, soft shadows, and a vibrant indigo primary color to guide the user's eye toward key conversion points. The design is dense enough to convey robust features but airy enough to feel effortless to use.

## Typography
- Headlines: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif — Bold, tightly tracked, geometric, and highly legible.
- Body: 'Inter', sans-serif — Clean, highly readable, relaxed line-height (1.6) for extended reading.

## Components
- Buttons: Medium border radius (rounded-md), solid fill for primary, subtle border for secondary. Hover states gently darken the background.
- Cards: White background (bg-background) on surface sections, soft slate borders (border-border), and subtle drop shadows (shadow-sm to shadow-md on hover).
- Sections: Generous vertical padding (py-16 to py-24). Alternating light (white) and surface (off-white/slate-50) backgrounds to delineate content blocks.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO use ample whitespace around headings to let the copy breathe.
- DO ensure all Call-To-Action buttons are highly visible using the primary color.
- DON'T clutter feature grids; keep icons, titles, and brief descriptions aligned.
- DON'T use full black; stick to the deep slate (hsl 222 47% 8%) for a softer, more premium dark look.
